# Privacy Policy — Blockio Blast

**Last updated:** September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Blockio Blast ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and
shares information when you play the App. Blockio Blast is developed by Mustafa Sahin
("the Developer").

## Summary

Blockio Blast does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address,
or any other personally identifying information to play. The App uses a small set of
third-party services to keep an anonymous leaderboard, process optional in-app purchases, and
(where enabled) show advertising. Each is described below.

## Information We Collect

### Anonymous player identifier
On first launch, the App signs you in anonymously through **Unity Authentication**
(a service of Unity Technologies). This creates a random player ID that is not linked to your
name, email, or any other personal account. This ID is used solely to:
- Save your progress and high scores between sessions on the same device.
- Submit scores to the App's leaderboards.

### Gameplay data
The App stores your scores, badges, and progress locally on your device and, via the anonymous
player ID above, on **Unity Leaderboards** so you can see how you rank against other players.

### Purchase data
If you make an in-app purchase (coin bundles, ad removal), the purchase is processed by
**Google Play Billing** through the **Unity In-App Purchasing** SDK. We receive a purchase
receipt to unlock the content you paid for; we do not receive or store your payment card
details — those are handled entirely by Google Play.

### Advertising data
Where the App shows rewarded or interstitial ads, this is done through **Google AdMob**.
AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), general device
information, and app usage data to select and measure ads, and may use this data consistent
with Google's own advertising and privacy policies. You can review Google's practices at
<https://policies.google.com/privacy> and, on supported devices, opt out of personalized
advertising or reset your advertising ID in your device's system settings.

### Automatically collected technical data
Unity Gaming Services and Google AdMob may automatically collect basic technical information
(device model, operating system version, general region, crash logs) to keep the services
running reliably and to diagnose problems. This is standard operational data and is not used to
identify you personally.

## Information We Do NOT Collect

- We do not ask for or store your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
- We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.
- We do not sell your information.

## Children's Privacy

Blockio Blast is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age
in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If
you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us using
the details below so we can address it.

## Your Choices

- You can reset or limit ad tracking through your device's system settings (Settings →
  Google → Ads, on most Android devices), which limits the advertising data AdMob can use for
  personalization.
- Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data from your device. Your anonymous
  leaderboard entry may persist on Unity's servers, tied only to the anonymous player ID, which
  cannot be linked back to you personally.
